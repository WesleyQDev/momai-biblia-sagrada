# Holy Bible for MomAI

The Holy Bible is an extension dedicated to reading, daily meditation, and studying the Holy Scriptures directly inside MomAI. Designed to uplift your Christian walk, it provides a pleasant, seamless, distraction-free devotional experience.

## A Blessing for Your Daily Walk with the Word of God

The Word of the Lord is a lamp unto our feet and a light unto our path. With this extension integrated into your desktop workflow, you can nourish your faith, search passages quickly, and keep your Bible reading always on track.

### Key Features

- **Complete Sacred Text**: Contains all 66 books of the Protestant Holy Bible (39 Old Testament books from Genesis to Malachi, and 27 New Testament books from Matthew to Revelation).
- **Faithful & Respected Edition**: Text based on the cherished João Ferreira de Almeida translation, widely recognized and revered.
- **Verse of the Day & Meditation**: Every time you open the extension, receive an inspiring passage with its full citation and a direct button to read the chapter context.
- **Instant Passage Search**: Easily locate any verse or book by natural citation (e.g. John 3:16, Psalm 23, Romans 8:28) or keyword topics.
- **Fluid & Comfortable Reading**: Navigate books and chapters effortlessly using the side index, bottom navigation arrows, or keyboard shortcuts.
- **Resume Where You Left Off**: Automatically remembers your last reading position so you never lose your reading plan.
- **Verse Bookmarks**: Highlight and save favorite verses, copy in one click, and share with your family or church groups.
- **Daily Goals Widget**: Track your sequential Bible reading directly on the MomAI widgets screen, featuring daily checklists, streak tracking, and instant chapter opening in the reader.
- **Complete Christian Harp**: All 640 hymns of the hymnal with an index from 1 to 640, search by number, title or lyric excerpt, favorites, and the same realistic book reader used for the Bible.
- **100% Offline**: All scripture texts are bundled locally. No internet connection required.
- **Study Companion with MomAI**: Ask your AI assistant to look up verses, explain passages, or assist in contextual Bible study.

## How to Use

1. Open MomAI and click the Holy Bible icon on the sidebar.
2. On the home view, read the daily verse or use the search bar to jump to any passage.
3. Open the book index to browse all 66 books and chapters.
4. Click any verse in reading mode to bookmark or copy it.

## Christian Harp

The extension home screen also brings the **complete Christian Harp** (*Harpa Cristã*): all 640 hymns of the Brazilian hymnal, with an index from 1 to 640, search by number, title or lyric excerpt, and favorites.

- **The same reading experience**: hymns open in the same realistic book used for the Bible, with page turning, two columns and side-index navigation.
- **Complete index**: find any hymn by number (e.g. 545), by title (e.g. "Because He Lives") or by a chorus excerpt.
- **Harp favorites**: bookmark the hymns you sing most and return to them anytime.
- **Resume at the last hymn**: the extension remembers the hymn you were on.
- **In your language, original in Portuguese**: title, stanzas and chorus follow the selected language (Settings, welcome screen or Languages button); Portuguese is the original and is used when a translation is missing.

To open it, click the Christian Harp button at the center of the four main icons on the home screen.
